I am a Ph.D. student in Informatics at Penn State University, advised by {{ site.author.advisor }}. I am currently a student researcher at Google DeepMind in New York, working on continual learning and memory for LLM agents. In summer 2025, I was a research scientist intern at Adobe Research. Before Penn State, I obtained my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Xidian University, where I ranked first in my class of 134.

My research develops scalable frameworks and algorithms that enable LLM agents to reason, collaborate, and self-improve in complex, open-ended environments. In particular, I study how to debug and optimize agentic behavior and how to form effective multi-agent teams. I am a co-creator and maintainer of AutoGen.
