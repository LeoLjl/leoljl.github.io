I am a Ph.D. student in Informatics at Penn State University, advised by {{ site.author.advisor }}, and a co-creator and maintainer of AutoGen. In 2026, I was a Student Researcher at Google DeepMind in New York, working on continual learning and memory mechanisms for LLM agents that learn from experience. In Summer 2025, I interned at Adobe Research, where I developed multi-agent systems for open-ended collaborative and creative workflows. Before Penn State, I obtained my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Xidian University, ranking first in my class of 134.

My research develops scalable frameworks and algorithms that enable LLM agents to reason, collaborate, and self-improve in complex, open-ended environments. In particular, I study how to debug and optimize agentic behavior and how to form effective multi-agent teams.
